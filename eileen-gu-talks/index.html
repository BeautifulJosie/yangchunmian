我承认，我爱上了恐惧I admit it: I'm in love with fear

2022-02-01 · 《纽约时报》· 北京冬奥前她亲笔写的随笔The New York Times · A first-person essay written before Beijing 2022

背景Context

北京冬奥开幕前几天，《纽约时报》刊出了一篇她自己写的文章，题目叫《我承认，我爱上了恐惧》。这不是采访，是她第一次系统地写下自己怎么理解恐惧。后来她在各种场合谈情绪、谈压力，几乎都能在这篇文章里找到源头。

Days before Beijing 2022, The New York Times ran an essay she wrote herself, titled I Admit It. I'm in Love With Fear. It is not an interview; it is the first time she set down systematically how she understands fear. Almost everything she has said since about emotions and pressure traces back to it.

她写了什么What she wrote

她把「恐惧」拆成三种不同的感觉：兴奋、不确定、压力。三种感觉要分开读：兴奋是燃料，不确定是受伤的预警，压力则取决于你怎么拿着它。把它们混成一个词叫「害怕」，就没法处理。

She breaks fear into three distinct sensations: excitement, uncertainty and pressure. Each has to be read differently: excitement is fuel, uncertainty is a warning of injury, and pressure depends on how you hold it. Lumping them into one word called fear makes them impossible to work with.

做一个新动作之前，她有一套固定流程，不是靠胆子：先在脑子里把每一个动作过一遍，深呼吸，闭眼，然后做一次有意识的风险评估——把自信和兴奋放在一边，把不确定放在另一边，看哪边重。

Before a new trick she runs a fixed routine, not bravado: visualise every movement, take a deep breath, close her eyes, then make a deliberate risk assessment, weighing confidence and excitement on one side against uncertainty on the other.

她很坦白地写道，不确定感很容易压过自信，带来的不是慌张，而是更接近「不祥的预感」。能识别出自己此刻处在哪种状态，才是决定要不要上的依据。

She writes frankly that uncertainty overrides confidence easily, and what it produces is less panic than something closer to dread. Recognising which state you are in is what the go or no-go decision rests on.

来自家庭、赞助商、对手的压力，可以帮她也可以害她。她的解法是把对外界认可的需求降下来：自尊、感激，以及真的享受这项运动本身。

Pressure from family, sponsors or rivals can help or hurt. Her remedy is to lower her need for outside approval: self-esteem, gratitude, and genuinely enjoying the sport itself.

她说自己和恐惧的关系还在变，但大概会一直是个「无可救药的浪漫主义者」——恐惧是伙伴，不是敌人。

Her relationship with fear is still evolving, she says, but she expects to stay a hopeless romantic about it: fear is a partner, not an enemy.

在中国我是中国人，在美国我是美国人Chinese in China, American in America

2022-02-08 · 北京冬奥会 · 自由式滑雪女子大跳台夺金后新闻发布会Beijing 2022 · Women's Freeski Big Air gold-medal press conference

背景Context

2022 年 2 月 8 日，谷爱凌在首钢大跳台最后一跳完成从未在比赛中做过的 1620，逆转夺金。几十分钟后，她坐进新闻发布厅，等着她的不是关于那一跳的技术问题，而是一个反复被问的问题：你还持有美国护照吗？

On 8 February 2022, Eileen Gu landed a 1620 she had never attempted in competition on her final jump at Big Air Shougang and took gold. Minutes later she sat down in the press room, where the questions were not about the jump but, again and again, about whether she still held a US passport.

这场发布会后来成了她公共生活里被审视最多的一个片段。她当时十八岁，面对的是来自两个国家的记者，各自带着各自的预设。

That press conference became the most scrutinised moment of her public life. She was 18, facing reporters from two countries, each side arriving with its own assumptions.

她说了什么What she said

先说那一跳。最后一轮之前，母亲建议她保守求稳，她没听，选择了训练里都没完成过的 1620——因为她想让世界看到自己的上限在哪里。落地后她先去安慰失误的对手，并把自己敢跳归功于对手把水平逼到了这个高度。

First, the jump. Before the final round her mother advised playing it safe. She did not, and went for a 1620 she had never landed even in training, because she wanted the world to see where her ceiling was. After landing she went first to console a rival who had fallen, and credited the rival's level for forcing the attempt.

她没有回答护照问题，而是把问题换了个框架：身份不是一张证件，而是你在哪里长大、和谁一起生活。在美国她是美国人，在中国她是中国人；她对两边的滑雪队都心怀感激。

She did not answer the passport question. Instead she reframed it: identity is not a document but where you grew up and whom you lived with. In the US she is American, in China she is Chinese, and she is grateful to both ski teams.

她把自己来北京比赛的原因讲得很具体：母亲是北京人，她从小每个夏天都回北京，这里是她的另一个家。她把这次参赛当作一次回家，而不是一次站队。

She was concrete about why she competes for China: her mother is from Beijing and she spent every childhood summer there. She framed the Games as a homecoming, not as choosing a side.

她反复回到一个使命：用自己的能见度把自由式滑雪带给更多年轻人，尤其是中国的女孩。在她看来，体育应该是连接国家的桥，而不是分割它们的线。

She kept returning to one mission: to use her visibility to bring freeskiing to more young people, especially girls in China. To her, sport should be a bridge between nations, not a line between them.

面对敌意，她的回应是提醒对方她的年龄：她是个十八岁的孩子。她还补了一句很有锋芒的话——那些批评她的人，永远不会知道拿到奥运金牌是什么感觉。

To hostility she answered with her age: she is an 18-year-old kid. And she added a line with an edge to it: her critics will never know what winning Olympic gold feels like.

对中国观众，她说得最多的一句不是关于赢，而是关于关系：她不想当偶像，更想做大家的朋友。被问到决赛时的压力，她说当时没有压力，现场一度安静下来——她把比赛动机定义为做最好的自己，而不是胜过别人。

To the Chinese audience, her most repeated line was not about winning but about the relationship: she did not want to be an idol, she wanted to be a friend. Asked about pressure in the final, she said there was none, and the room went quiet for a moment. She defined her motivation as being her best self rather than beating anyone.

为什么值得听Why it is worth hearing

这场发布会定下了她此后四年处理「中美问题」的模板：不正面回答法律问题，把对话拉回文化和经历，再把焦点转向她想推动的事。无论你是否认同这种策略，它在一个十八岁的人身上出现，本身就值得一看。

This press conference set the template she has used ever since for the US–China question: decline the legal question, pull the conversation back to culture and lived experience, then redirect to what she wants to build. Whether or not you agree with the strategy, seeing it formed in an 18-year-old is worth the watch.

她把滑雪和奥运看成一种国际关系：运动让人用同一种语言分享快乐与痛苦。这也是她不回避舆论中心、坚持做真实自己的理由。

She sees skiing and the Olympics as a kind of international relations: sport lets people share joy and pain in one language. That is also why she does not step away from the centre of attention and insists on being herself.

最大的对手是自己The biggest rival is yourself

2026-04-01 · 第四届「巾帼建新力论坛」主旨分享（香港信德中心）· 香港各界妇女联合协进会主办Keynote at the 4th Women's Innovation Forum, Shun Tak Centre, Hong Kong · hosted by the Hong Kong Federation of Women

背景Context

米兰之后一个多月，她在香港和何超琼、郭晶晶同台，做了一场 15 分钟的主旨分享。这是她第一次在中文语境下以「女性领导力」而不是「运动员」的身份发言。

A little over a month after Milan she shared a Hong Kong stage with Pansy Ho and Guo Jingjing for a 15-minute keynote. It was her first time speaking in a Chinese-language setting as a voice on women's leadership rather than as an athlete.

她说了什么What she said

最大的竞争对手不是别人，而是自己；比赛不只为奖牌，更为突破。

The biggest rival is not anyone else but yourself; competing is about breakthroughs, not only medals.

在一项曾被视为男性专属的运动里成为奖牌最多的人，对她有特殊意义，她还想创造更多「历史第一」。她举了 2021 年成为第一个完成 1440 的女选手的例子：第一个人把门打开，后面的女性路会好走一些。

Becoming the most decorated athlete in a sport once seen as men's territory means something particular to her, and she wants more firsts. She cited being the first woman to land a 1440 in 2021: the first person opens the door, and the road is a little easier for the women who follow.

帮助年轻女性的方式，不是替她们定义什么「不适合」，而是扩大她们的选择，让她们自己定义。

The way to help young women is not to define for them what does not suit them, but to widen their choices and let them define it themselves.

她说女性天生多面，拥有共情、倾听、联结和凝聚人心的力量。六枚奥运奖牌在手，她仍然说自己年轻，仍在学习。

Women, she said, are naturally many-sided, with a particular strength in empathy, listening, connecting and bringing people together. With six Olympic medals, she still called herself young and still learning.

像从未赢过那样训练，像从未输过那样比赛Train like you've never won, compete like you've never lost

2026-08-31 · 播客《On Purpose with Jay Shetty》·「The Ground Was Cracking Beneath Me」（65 分钟）On Purpose with Jay Shetty · 'The Ground Was Cracking Beneath Me' (65 min)

背景Context

米兰之后半年，她坐进 Jay Shetty 的播客，聊了一个多小时。这期节目的视频版在 Netflix 上线，音频在 Spotify、Apple Podcasts 都能听。它不谈具体比赛，谈的是她怎么训练自己的头脑：方向、证据、批评，以及如何把「自己」和「自己的手艺」分开。

Six months after Milan she sat down with Jay Shetty for just over an hour. The video version streams on Netflix, the audio on Spotify and Apple Podcasts. It is not about any particular competition; it is about how she trains her mind: direction, evidence, criticism, and keeping herself separate from her craft.

她说了什么What she said

她首先把自己定义为一个选择了体育的知识分子，而不是一个碰巧重视智力的运动员。队里的人都知道她是「最大的书呆子」，经常帮队友做作业。

She defines herself first as an intellectual who chose sport, not an athlete who happens to value intelligence. Her teammates knew her as the biggest nerd on the team, the one who helped with homework.

关于努力，她说了一句很反直觉的话：努力被高估了。朝错误的方向拼命，就像用力打一堵墙——力气很大，进展很少；方向对了，努力才会复利。母亲教她的是「怎么工作」比「比别人更拼」重要。

On effort she said something counter-intuitive: hard work is over-praised. Working hard in the wrong direction is like punching a wall: lots of force, little progress. With the right direction, effort compounds. What her mother taught her was that how you work matters more than out-working everyone.

她把学习称为终极超能力：相信自己能把任何事弄明白。十二三岁时她就开始读育儿书，为了理解自己的成长；移民母亲的「学生心态」——承认自己不知道——是她学来的。

She calls learning the ultimate superpower: the confidence that you can figure anything out. At 12 or 13 she started reading parenting books to understand her own development, and her immigrant mother's student mentality, admitting what she did not know, is where she learned it.

这期最常被引用的，是她和朋友之间的「建设性批评圈」。它是一个正式的约定：朋友之间直接、坦率地指出彼此的弱点，不带自尊心进去。目的是快速进步——批评只要不往心里去，就能马上用上。她把指出对方的短处看作一种深层的爱与服务，前提是它来自支持而不是羞辱。

The most quoted part of the episode is her constructive criticism circle with friends. It is a formal arrangement: friends point out each other's weaknesses openly and directly, with ego left outside. The point is speed of improvement; criticism you do not take personally can be applied at once. She sees naming a friend's weakness as a deep act of love and service, as long as it comes from support rather than insult.

训练和比赛，她用两套完全不同的心态。训练时她对自己极度挑剔——转体差了两度、起跳早了一秒都要盯着，防止自满；比赛时则切换成彻底的自信，相信自己是场上最好的，其他人在争第二。这种巅峰心态只在上场那一刻打开，平时带着它会养出危险的自我。

For training and competing she uses two completely different mindsets. In training she is hypercritical, fixating on a rotation two degrees short or a take-off a second early, to keep complacency out. In competition she switches to total confidence: she is the best on the hill and everyone else is fighting for second. That peak mindset is opened only for the performance; carried around every day it would breed a dangerous ego.

信念不能靠口号假装出来。她的说法是「证据胜过肯定」：信任只能靠重复和真正的准备一点点攒出来。可视化是其中一部分：她会从多个机位、快放慢放地在脑子里预演动作，甚至模拟 G 力。

Belief cannot be faked with affirmations. Her phrase is evidence over affirmation: trust is built only through repetition and real preparation. Visualisation is part of it; she rehearses tricks from several camera angles, in slow and fast motion, even simulating the G-forces.

她也讲了那一个小时的事：米兰夺金后几分钟，还没上领奖台，她看到了殡仪馆的邮件，知道外婆走了。金牌一下子变得不重要。她把悲伤转成了做事——接手葬礼的安排，然后在最后一场发布会上把这件事告诉了大家。

She also told the story of that hour: minutes after winning gold in Milan, before the medal ceremony, she saw an email from a funeral home and knew her grandmother was gone. The gold suddenly felt trivial. She turned grief into work, taking on the funeral arrangements, and then told the world at her final press conference.

为什么值得听Why it is worth hearing

如果你只想从她身上学一个可操作的习惯，是这一章：找几个信得过的朋友，约定互相直说。它把前面几章里反复出现的「意图」「尝试」「勇敢」落到了一个具体的做法上。

If you want one practical habit from her, it is in this chapter: find a few trusted friends and agree to tell each other the truth. It turns the intention, trying and bravery of earlier chapters into a concrete practice.

做你自己的那条鱼Be your own fish

2026-10-12 · 新加坡管理大学 · Karim 家族基金会「启发女性领导力」系列 · 与校长 Lily Kong 的炉边对谈Singapore Management University · Karim Family Foundation Inspiring Women Leadership Series · Fireside chat with President Lily Kong

背景Context

米兰之后八个月，她在新加坡面对一千多名听众，做了一场 41 分钟的对谈。话题从滑雪移开了：领导力、教育、女性、人工智能。这是她到目前为止最新的一次长时间公开对话，也显示了她 2026 年之后的转向。

Eight months after Milan she spoke for 41 minutes to an audience of over a thousand in Singapore. The subject had moved off skiing: leadership, education, women, artificial intelligence. It is her most recent long public conversation and shows where she is heading after 2026.

她说了什么What she said

人的潜能是可以训练的。她用神经可塑性来解释：心态和习惯和肌肉一样，靠重复塑造。

Human potential is trainable. She explained it through neuroplasticity: mindset and habits, like muscle, are shaped by repetition.

自我觉察从观察当下的情绪开始：分清楚此刻是恐惧、怀疑还是紧张，才能掌控成长的方向。

Self-awareness starts with observing the emotion of the moment: telling fear from doubt from nerves is what lets you steer your own growth.

竞争的意义是互相推着变好，是让人敢于尝试。在可能失败的时候第一个上，比赢更重要。

Competition is about pushing each other to be better and feeling empowered to try. Going first when failure is possible matters more than winning.

谈女性进步，她的框架是整个群体一起上升——水涨船高——而不是某一个人的明星化。

On women's advancement her frame was the whole community rising together, a rising tide lifting all ships, rather than individual stardom.

标题那句话的来历：不要在「大池小鱼」和「小池大鱼」之间选，建自己的池塘，做自己想做的那条鱼，停止和别人比。起点不利的时候，与其盯着手里的牌，不如把牌打好。

Where the title comes from: do not choose between big fish in a small pond and small fish in a big pond. Build your own pond, be the fish you want to be, and stop comparing. When the starting hand is poor, play it well rather than stare at it.

归属感来自哪里？她说来自小时候作为队里唯一的女孩，在雪道上一次次艰难时刻里挣来的友谊。

Where does belonging come from? From being the only girl on her youth team, she said, and earning friendship through hard moments on the slopes.

关于 AI，她的态度是把它当作一股向善的力量而不是威胁；而要用好它，需要的是判断力和会提好问题。

On AI she takes it as a force for good rather than something to fear, and says using it well takes judgment and good questions.

最后是母亲：小时候给她选择，长大后给她自由。她现在称她们两人为「联合创始人」。

And her mother: choices when she was small, freedom when she grew up. She now calls the two of them co-founders.

为什么值得听Why it is worth hearing

如果前八章是一个运动员在学习怎么说话，这一章是她开始用这套话语谈体育以外的事。「做你自己的那条鱼」这个标题，和 2022 年发布会上那个被追问护照的女孩放在一起看，会很有意思。

If the first eight chapters show an athlete learning how to speak, this one shows her using that vocabulary for things beyond sport. Read the title, be your own fish, next to the girl grilled about her passport in 2022, and the distance is the point.
